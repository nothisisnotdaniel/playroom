# 004 Move History and Health behind a More menu in the tab bar

As a phone user, I want fewer, larger tabs in PlayRoom's bottom bar, so each one is easy to hit.

Category: ui

## What

Replace the History and Health tabs with a More button that opens a small menu with both, as in ControlRoom #179 (tab-more and tab-more-menu in index.html, the more-menu functions in app.js). Jobs, Games, Backlogs and Capture stay as tabs. The menu closes on a pick, on Escape and on a tap outside it, and the More tab shows as current while History or Health is open.

## Why

PlayRoom has six tabs at phone width, more than ControlRoom's four plus More, so the labels get cramped.
